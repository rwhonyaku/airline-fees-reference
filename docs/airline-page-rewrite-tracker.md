## Batch standard

Each completed page should have:

1. A query-matched H1 and verdict that answer the cost decision quickly.
2. Airline-specific traps and tactics rather than policy paraphrases.
3. Concrete traveler scenarios that expose the airline's fee logic.
4. Explicit exceptions and uncertainty where rules depend on route or operator.
5. Specific metadata and useful links into fee guides or deterministic tools.
6. No unsupported enforcement claims or invented prices.
